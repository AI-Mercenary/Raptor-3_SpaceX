    How to read this

    The slice is a vertical cut through the engine axis, mirrored left/right. Each pixel is one solver cell coloured by the selected field. Still ambient air is left transparent so only the jet shows.

    Temperature: chamber ≈3,550 K, cooling as the gas expands; it re-heats at shocks. Mach: <1 in the chamber, exactly 1 at the throat, ≈3.9 at the exit. Pressure: log scale — 350 bar in the chamber down to ~1 bar. Schlieren: dark = sharp density change, i.e. shock waves and the jet boundary.

    The graph shows Mach, temperature and pressure along the centre-line from injector (left) to plume (right). Saw-tooth wiggles after the exit are the shock cells. Residual measures how much the solution is still changing — lower means more converged.

    Model: inviscid (no viscosity/turbulence), single gas with γ=1.2, no chemistry or afterburning, 2-D axisymmetric. Good for shock structure and nozzle performance; not a substitute for full reacting 3-D CFD.
